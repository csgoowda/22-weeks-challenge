## 1. What Is a Percentage?

Percentage means a value out of 100.

The symbol is `%`.

For example:

50% = 50/100 = 0.5

If you score 50 marks out of 100, your percentage is 50%.

---

## 2. Fraction to Percentage

**Formula:**

Percentage = (Numerator / Denominator) × 100

### Example 1:

Convert 5/10 into a percentage.

Solution:

= (5/10) × 100

= 50%

**Answer: 50%**

### Example 2:

Convert 5000/20000 into a percentage.

Solution:

= (5000/20000) × 100

Step 1: Multiply the numerator by 100.

= (5000 × 100) / 20000

Step 2: Cancel the zeros.

= 500000 / 20000

= 25%

**Answer: 25%**

### Example 3:

Convert 3/4 into a percentage.

Solution:

= (3/4) × 100

= 75%

**Answer: 75%**

### Useful Fraction Conversions to Remember

| Fraction | Percentage |
|---|---|
| 1/2 | 50% |
| 1/3 | 33.33% |
| 1/4 | 25% |
| 1/5 | 20% |
| 1/10 | 10% |
| 3/4 | 75% |

## 3. Percentage to Fraction

Divide the percentage by 100 and simplify.

### Example 1:

Convert 25% into a fraction.

Solution:

= 25/100

= 1/4

**Answer: 1/4**

### Example 2:

Convert 75% into a fraction.

Solution:

= 75/100

= 3/4

**Answer: 3/4**

---

## 4. Decimal to Percentage

**Formula:**

Percentage = Decimal × 100

### Examples:

Example 1:

0.5 × 100 = 50%

Example 2:

0.25 × 100 = 25%

Example 3:

0.75 × 100 = 75%

---

## 5. Percentage to Decimal

Divide the percentage by 100.

### Examples:

Example 1:

50% / 100 = 0.5

Example 2:

25% / 100 = 0.25

Example 3:

7.5% / 100 = 0.075

---

## 6. Find X% of a Number

**Formula:**

X% of N = (X / 100) × N

### Example 1: Find 20% of 500.

Solution:

= (20/100) × 500

= 100

**Answer: 100**

### Example 2: Find 15% of 200.

Solution:

= (15/100) × 200

= 30

**Answer: 30**

---

## 7. Quick Calculation Tricks

| Percentage | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| 10% | Divide by 10 |
| 20% | Divide by 5 |
| 25% | Divide by 4 |
| 50% | Divide by 2 |
| 5% | Find 10%, then divide by 2 |
| 1% | Divide by 100 |

### Example: Find 25% of 800.

Shortcut:

800 / 4 = 200

**Answer: 200**

This is faster than using the long formula.

A. 25%  
B. 50%  
C. 75%

3 / 4 × 100 = 75%

### Q3. Convert 0.25 into a percentage.

A. 2.5%  
B. 25%  
C. 250%

0.25 × 100 = 25%

**Correct Answer: B. 25%**

A. 6  
B. 0.06  
C. 0.6  
D. 60

**Solution:**

60 / 100 = 0.6

**Correct Answer: C. 0.6**

### Q5. Find 10% of 500.

A. 5  
B. 50  
C. 100  
D. 250

**Solution:**

500 / 10 = 50

**Correct Answer: B. 50**

### Q6. Find 25% of 800.

A. 100  
B. 150  
C. 200  
D. 250

**Solution:**

800 / 4 = 200

**Correct Answer: C. 200**

### Q7. Find 20% of 150.

A. 15  
B. 20  
C. 30  
D. 50

**Solution:**

150 / 5 = 30

**Correct Answer: C. 30**

A. 20%  
B. 25%  
C. 40%  
D. 50%

**Solution:**

(5000 / 20000) × 100 = 25%

**Correct Answer: B. 25%**

### Q9. Convert 75% into a fraction in simplest form.

A. 1/2  
B. 1/4  
C. 3/4  
D. 4/5

**Correct Answer: C. 3/4**

B. 20
