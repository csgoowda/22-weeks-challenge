# Day 1 – Aptitude: Percentages

## 1. Important Percentage Formulas

1. Percentage = (Part / Total) × 100
2. Find a Value = (Percentage / 100) × Total
3. Find Total = (Part × 100) / Percentage
4. Percentage Increase = (Increase / Original Value) × 100
5. Percentage Decrease = (Decrease / Original Value) × 100

---

## 2. Practice Questions with Solutions

### Question 1: Find 20% of 500.

**Solution:**

Step 1: Write the formula.

20/100 × 500

Step 2: Simplify.

20 × 5 = 100

**Answer: 100**

---

### Question 2: Find 15% of 800.

**Solution:**

15/100 × 800

15 × 8 = 120

**Answer: 120**

---

### Question 3: 40 is what percentage of 200?

**Solution:**

Formula:

Percentage = (Part / Total) × 100

Substitute the values:

(40/200) × 100

0.2 × 100 = 20%

**Answer: 20%**

---

### Question 4: A student scored 360 marks out of 500. Find the percentage.

**Solution:**

Percentage = (360/500) × 100

\= 0.72 × 100

\= 72%

**Answer: 72%**

---

### Question 5: The price of a product is ₹1,000. It increases by 20%. Find the new price.

**Solution:**

Step 1: Calculate the increase.

20/100 × 1000 = 200

Step 2: Add the increase to the original price.

1000 + 200 = 1200

**Answer: ₹1,200**

---

### Question 6: A product costs ₹800. Its price decreases by 25%. Find the new price.

**Solution:**

Step 1: Calculate the decrease.

25/100 × 800 = 200

Step 2: Subtract the decrease.

800 - 200 = 600

**Answer: ₹600**

---

### Question 7: A student scored 45 marks, which is 75% of the total marks. Find the total marks.

**Solution:**

Formula:

Total = (Part × 100) / Percentage

Substitute:

(45 × 100) / 75

4500 / 75 = 60

**Answer: 60 marks**

---

### Question 8: A salary increases from ₹20,000 to ₹25,000. Find the percentage increase.

**Solution:**

Step 1: Find the increase.

25000 - 20000 = 5000

Step 2: Calculate the percentage increase.

(5000/20000) × 100

\= 25%

**Answer: 25%**

---

### Question 9: A number is decreased by 10% and becomes 450. Find the original number.

**Solution:**

After a 10% decrease, 90% remains.

90% of the original number = 450

Original number = (450 × 100) / 90

\= 500

**Answer: 500**

---

### Question 10: A product costs ₹2,000. Its price increases by 10%. Find the new price.

**Solution:**

Step 1: Calculate the increase.

10/100 × 2000 = 200

Step 2: Add the increase.

2000 + 200 = 2200

**Answer: ₹2,200**
