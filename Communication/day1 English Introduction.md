# Day 1 – English Introduction

15 minutes

Speaking practice

Your task is to prepare and practise a two-minute self-introduction for software developer interviews.

## 1. Why is self-introduction important?

Interviewers often begin with:

"Tell me about yourself."

Your introduction should explain:

1. Your educational background.
2. Your technical background.
3. Your manufacturing experience.
4. Your software development interests.
5. Your career goal.

## 2. Your sample introduction

Here is a simple version based on your academic and technical background.

Good morning.

My name is Chethan. I am currently pursuing my Bachelor of Engineering in Information Science and Engineering at SJCE, Mysuru.

Before joining engineering, I completed a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering from GTTC, Maddur. I also gained exposure to manufacturing and process design through my industrial experience.

This background helped me understand manufacturing processes and the challenges faced by industries.

I am interested in software development, especially Python backend development and industrial software solutions.

I have been learning Python, SQL, backend development and problem-solving. I am also working on a Smart Manufacturing Production and Resource Optimization System, which focuses on production scheduling and resource allocation.

My goal is to combine my manufacturing knowledge with software development skills and build useful solutions for industrial applications.

Thank you.

## 3. Understand these useful English sentences

| Sentence                     | Purpose                    |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| I am currently pursuing...   | Explain your education     |
| I completed my diploma in... | Explain previous education |
| I gained exposure to...      | Explain experience         |
| I am interested in...        | Explain your interests     |
| My goal is to...             | Explain career ambitions   |

## 4. Speaking practice

Follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction slowly.
2. Understand each sentence.
3. Speak without looking at the text.
4. Record your voice on your phone.
5. Listen and identify one sentence you can improve.
6. Repeat the introduction.

Important: Don't try to speak fast. Focus on clear pronunciation and simple grammar.
